# 🤖 AI-Generated Reference & Sparring Suite
### *PICT Knight Shift // Offline Training & Benchmark Partners*

This directory houses **7 pre-built AI chess bots** generated by LLM architectures. They serve as offline sparring partners, regression tests, and style archetypes for students developing their own engines.

---

## 🥊 The 7 Sparring Archetypes

| Bot | Est. Elo | Archetype & Search Technique | Weakness to Exploit |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **`charlie_chaos_bot.py`** | ~400 | Random legal mover. | Completely blind to threats. |
| **`bob_blitz_bot.py`** | ~800 | 1-ply greedy captures + material valuation. | Walks into simple deflection traps. |
| **`alice_alpha_bot.py`** | ~1300 | 2-ply classic Alpha-Beta with basic material sum. | Drops pieces to horizon effects (no quiescence). |
| **`boris_bastion_bot.py`** | ~1700 | Iron Bastion. High pawn-shield evaluation, fortress building, trades when ahead. | Struggles with dynamic pawn breaks and sacrificial gambits. |
| **`valkyrie_tactics_bot.py`** | ~1750 | Tactical Berserker. King-hunter engine with king-zone pressure and deep check quiescence. | Can overextend and compromise its own king safety. |
| **`victor_vanguard_bot.py`** | ~1800 | Positional Master. Aspiration-window Negamax, Zobrist TT, history heuristic, mobility bonuses. | Can struggle with sudden chaotic non-standard Chess960 setups. |
| **`varad_bot.py`** | ~1800 | Championship All-Rounder. Tapered procedural evaluation, passed-pawn advancement, dual killers, Triathlon specialist. | Vulnerable to extreme tactical sacrificial lines. |

---

## 🛠️ How to Spar Against These Bots Locally

Before submitting to `hackathon/submitted_bots/`, test your bot against these archetypes:

```bash
# 1. Quick 2-game sparring test vs Boris the Fortress:
python3 hackathon/arena_runner.py your_name_bot.py hackathon/ai_sparring_bots/boris_bastion_bot.py

# 2. Test your tactical defense vs Valkyrie the Berserker:
python3 hackathon/arena_runner.py your_name_bot.py hackathon/ai_sparring_bots/valkyrie_tactics_bot.py

# 3. Test your engine against the ~1800 Positional Master (Victor):
python3 hackathon/arena_runner.py your_name_bot.py hackathon/ai_sparring_bots/victor_vanguard_bot.py
```
